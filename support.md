# Traced Support

Traced is provided by Errant Step. For help, bug reports, purchase questions, or privacy requests, email
[errantstep@gmail.com](mailto:errantstep@gmail.com).

For a bug report, include your Traced version, Android version, device model, and steps that reproduce the problem.
Send drawings or screenshots only if you are comfortable sharing their contents. We do not have remote access to your
drawings.

## Support purchase

The optional one-time support purchase hides the free-version button. All drawing features remain available for free.
To restore a purchase, use the Google account that bought it and the restore action in the purchase prompt. Include
your Google Play order reference if you contact us about a payment; never send payment-card details.

## Remove your data

Traced has no app accounts. To remove local app data, use Android Settings > Apps > Traced > Storage > Clear storage,
or uninstall Traced. Device labels may differ. Save any projects held in private app storage to another location first.

Projects and exports in a shared folder or cloud document provider remain after uninstalling. Delete them using your
file manager or provider, including any backups or copies you shared. Manage Android backups through your device's
backup settings. Clearing app storage does not delete Google Play purchase records.

To request access, correction, or deletion of support correspondence held by Errant Step, email us. See the
[Traced Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.html) for data handling and retention details.
