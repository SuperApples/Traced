# Traced Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 November 2026

Traced is a drawing and architectural markup app provided by Errant Step. This policy explains how the Android app
(`com.errantstep.traced`) handles information. For privacy questions or requests, contact
[errantstep@gmail.com](mailto:errantstep@gmail.com).

## Drawings and files

Traced processes your drawings, imported images, and PDFs on your device. It accesses files and folders you select
through Android's system document picker. A folder selection can grant ongoing read and write access to that folder
so Traced can reopen and save your projects.

Projects contain your drawing content and may include imported backgrounds. Traced saves projects in your selected
folder, or in private app storage when no folder has been selected. New sketches are saved automatically, and projects
in the app's managed folder continue autosaving there. A separate private recovery copy helps restore the current
drawing after an interruption. Traced also stores recent-project references, preview images, and preferences such as
colours and pen settings to make the app work.

Errant Step does not operate a server that receives your drawings or provide its own cloud storage. If you choose a
cloud document provider, saving or autosaving to that location can upload the project through that provider. Its
storage, security, synchronisation, and retention practices apply. If you share an export, the app or recipient you
choose receives that file and handles it under its own policies.

## Optional support purchase

Traced offers an optional one-time support purchase through Google Play. All drawing features are available without
paying. Google processes the payment; Traced does not receive your payment-card details.

The app uses purchase information returned by Google Play, including the product, purchase status, and purchase token,
to recognise, acknowledge, and restore the purchase. It stores an ownership flag privately so the purchase remains
recognised offline. Errant Step does not send purchase tokens to a developer-operated server. Google Play may make
transaction information available to Errant Step through its developer and payments tools.

Google Play and its billing library handle purchase information and send technical billing diagnostics to Google to
operate, troubleshoot, and improve the billing service. These diagnostics include billing API results and errors,
timings, a randomly generated billing-client session identifier, app and library versions, Android version, device
brand/model/manufacturer/build and memory capacity, language and country settings, time zone, and network/carrier
information. Diagnostic uploads use HTTPS and may be queued in private app storage for later delivery or retry.

Billing can communicate with Google when the app connects to the service, including when checking existing purchases
without starting a new purchase. Traced has no setting to turn off these billing diagnostics. Google controls retention
of information it receives under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Your drawings are not
sent to Google Play Billing.

## Backups and diagnostics

Android may back up or transfer private app data according to your device settings and backup service. This can include
preferences, recent-project references, preview images, and projects saved in private storage. Traced's backup rules
exclude the private recovery autosave, its temporary file, and the cached support-purchase ownership flag from Android
cloud backup and device transfer. Projects and exports saved with a document provider follow that provider's backup
policy instead.

Traced writes operational and performance diagnostics to the local Android system log. It does not include a
developer-operated analytics, advertising, or telemetry service. Android and Google Play may separately collect
diagnostics under their own settings and policies and make crash or performance reports available to the developer.

## Contacting us

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer
your request, investigate problems, or handle privacy inquiries. Our email service provider, Google Gmail, processes
that correspondence. Please send only information needed for your request.

We keep support correspondence while it is needed to resolve and follow up on your request, and where required for
legal obligations or disputes. You can ask us to delete correspondence; we will explain any information we must retain.

## Retention and deletion

- Project files and exports remain until you delete them. Files in shared storage or a document provider remain after
  uninstalling Traced; delete those files and any provider backups separately.
- Private projects, recovery data, previews, preferences, and cached purchase ownership remain until replaced, deleted
  by the app, or removed by clearing app storage or uninstalling. Save any private projects elsewhere first. Restoring
  an Android backup may restore eligible data; manage existing backups through your backup provider.
- Sharing creates a temporary export in the app's cache. Exports older than a day are eligible for cleanup when you
  next share, rather than being deleted on a fixed timer. Android may also clear cached files. Copies already sent to
  another app or recipient are outside Traced's control.
- Clearing app storage or uninstalling does not erase Google's purchase records. Google controls its billing records
  and diagnostics under its own retention policies, and purchase ownership can be restored from Google Play.

Traced has no app accounts to create or delete. A Google account used for payment or a document provider is managed by
that provider. Errant Step cannot remotely access or delete drawings that remain on your device or with your chosen
provider. For information we hold, including support correspondence, email us with access, correction, or deletion
requests. You may also have rights under applicable privacy laws and may contact your local privacy regulator.

## Security and other services

Traced uses Android's private app storage for internal data and Android's document access and sharing mechanisms for
files you select. Project and export files are not encrypted by Traced itself; their protection depends on your device
and chosen storage provider. Keep sensitive drawings in a location with appropriate access controls and backups.

We do not sell personal information or use your drawings or support messages for advertising. Information you send us
may be processed by our service providers or disclosed where required by law. Google and your chosen providers may
process information outside your country under their own policies.

If you read these pages on GitHub Pages, GitHub processes website request information to serve and secure the pages
under its [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
These pages do not add analytics or advertising scripts.
